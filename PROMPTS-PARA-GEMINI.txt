OJO CON ESTA: el juego pone tres cosas encima en sitios EXACTOS (el
personaje y el texto de cada maquina). Si el dibujo las pone en otro sitio,
el jugador aparece de pie sobre una pared o al lado de la maquina que no es.

Create a LANDSCAPE image in 8:5 aspect ratio (a bit wider than tall), not
square.
Top-down interior of a small gym, seen from DIRECTLY ABOVE at a perfect
90-degree overhead angle - a floor-plan view, like the interiors in GTA
Chinatown Wars. The camera is mounted on the ceiling looking straight down
at the floor. NO walls seen from the front, NO perspective, NO tilt, NO
vanishing point.

FORMAT: landscape 8:5. The floor fills the entire image edge to edge, no
border, no margin.

THE FLOOR: dark rubber gym flooring with a grid pattern.

LAYOUT - this part matters, the game places a piece of equipment and a
label at each exact spot below, and nothing else should be drawn there:
- AT 20% of the width, 42% of the height: a weight bench with a barbell
  rack, seen from above.
- AT 50% of the width, 30% of the height: a treadmill, seen from above.
- AT 80% of the width, 42% of the height: a round punching bag hanging
  from the ceiling, seen from above as a dark circle.
- BOTTOM CENTRE EDGE (around 50% of the width, from 95% of the height to
  the bottom edge): a stretch of floor left completely clear and open,
  that is the door to the street.
- The rest of the floor (corners, the band above the equipment) can carry
  gym atmosphere: a wall mirror along the top edge, a water cooler, a
  rack of dumbbells in a corner - but nothing blocking the three pieces of
  equipment or the bottom-centre doorway.

LIGHT: bright, slightly cool gym lighting, fluorescent tube reflections on
the rubber floor.
COLOUR: dark grey and black rubber floor, muted red and steel gym
equipment, one warm accent (amber) somewhere in the decor.
STYLE: hand-painted 2D game art, bold dark outlines, flat shading. NOT
photorealistic, NOT a 3D render, NOT isometric.
MUST NOT CONTAIN: NO text, letters, numbers, painted words, signs with
readable writing or watermark. NO people. Nothing on top of the three
pieces of equipment or the bottom-centre doorway.
