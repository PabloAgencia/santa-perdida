#  EL CONCESIONARIO POR DENTRO (26-sep-2026)                     ES 1 PROMPT
################################################################################

Nuevo desde esta noche: el concesionario dejo de ser coches aparcados en la
acera y ahora se entra, como un piso. La sala YA esta montada en el juego
(seis plazas en fila con nombre y precio debajo, puerta abajo en el centro);
solo falta el suelo del salon.

OJO CON ESTA, es distinta a las ocho de arriba: NO lleva ni un mueble
dibujado, y TAMPOCO coches. El juego pone el sprite de cada coche de verdad
encima de cada plaza (el mismo dibujo del concesionario de antes, ahora aqui
dentro) — si el cuadro trae coches pintados, se ven dos superpuestos, como
pasaria con gente si el dibujo trajera gente.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  9.  interior-concesionario.png
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Un salon de coches: un solo espacio diafano y ancho, sin muebles.

Top-down interior of a wide car dealership showroom floor, seen from
DIRECTLY ABOVE at a perfect 90-degree overhead angle - a floor-plan view,
like the interiors in GTA Chinatown Wars. The camera is mounted on the
ceiling looking straight down at the floor. NO walls seen from the front,
NO perspective, NO tilt, NO vanishing point.

FORMAT: landscape, 1600 x 720 pixels, wide and shallow (roughly 2.2:1). The
floor fills the entire image edge to edge, no border, no margin.

THE FLOOR: polished glossy showroom concrete or pale tile, reflective,
almost empty on purpose — this is a car salon, not a room to furnish.

LAYOUT - this part matters, the game places six car sprites and a door on
top at these exact spots, and nothing else should be drawn there:

- A ROW OF SIX EMPTY DISPLAY BAYS evenly spaced left to right, centred
  roughly 44% of the way down from the top edge. Each bay is a distinct
  circular or rounded-square lit platform / showroom spotlight pool on the
  floor (like a spotlit parking spot), clearly separate from its neighbours,
  but EMPTY - no car, no shape resembling a vehicle, nothing standing on it.
- BOTTOM CENTRE EDGE: a stretch of floor left completely clear and open,
  that is the door to the street.
- The rest of the floor (top band above the bays, the gaps between them,
  the corners) can carry dealership atmosphere: floor reflections, subtle
  directional light streaks, maybe a distant reception counter or a neon
  sign shape against the very top edge - but nothing blocking the six
  bays or the bottom-centre doorway, and no vehicles anywhere.

LIGHT: bright, cool showroom lighting, a spotlight glow over each of the six
bays, reflections on the glossy floor.
COLOUR: cool grey and white floor, blue-white spotlights, maybe one accent
colour (amber or cyan) in the distant signage. Clean and commercial, not
grimy like the hideouts.
STYLE: hand-painted 2D game art, bold dark outlines, flat shading. NOT
photorealistic, NOT a 3D render, NOT isometric.
MUST NOT CONTAIN: NO text, letters, numbers, painted words, signs with
readable writing or watermark. NO people. NO cars, motorbikes or any vehicle
shape anywhere, not even in the background. Nothing on top of the six bays.
